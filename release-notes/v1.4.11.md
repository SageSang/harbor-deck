## 中文

- 分组与书签管理新增按场景导出浏览器书签。
- 导出的 HTML 保留一级分组和空分组；有备用链接时优先导出备用链接，快速记录会作为根目录书签导出。
- 导出的文件可以直接导入 Chrome 和 Edge。

## English

- Add browser bookmark export by scene to Groups & Bookmarks management.
- Exported HTML preserves one-level groups and empty groups, prefers secondary URLs, and keeps quick records as root-level bookmarks.
- Exported files can be imported directly into Chrome and Edge.
