## 中文

- 首页分组现在可以通过拖动把手调整顺序，支持跨行移动和移动到列表末尾。
- 分组排序会持久化保存，并与书签拖拽交互保持隔离。
- 优化大量分组场景下的排序稳定性，以及桌面端和移动端的分组标题布局。

## English

- Reorder homepage groups by dragging their dedicated handle, including cross-row moves and moving a group to the end of the list.
- Group ordering is persisted and kept isolated from bookmark drag-and-drop interactions.
- Improved ordering stability with many groups and refined group-title layout on desktop and mobile.
